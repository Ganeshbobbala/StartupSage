# StartupSage: Phase 1 ("Feel It") - Intern Overview

Welcome to the StartupSage team! This document outlines **exactly** what we are building over the next 8 weeks for Phase 1. 

## The Goal
**Get students to complete one full simulation and share their Founder Passport.**
We want a student who has never thought about starting a company to finish a 20-minute simulation and immediately want to share what they learned. Everything else is noise.

---

## What Ships in Phase 1

### 1. The Core Simulation (8 Stages)
This is a "lean" version of the simulation. Each stage consists of: 
*   One screen of context
*   One decision with 2-3 options
*   One consequence screen
*(Total playtime target: 20–25 minutes)*

*   **Stage 0: The Spark**
    *   User picks a problem domain from 6 options (HealthTech, EdTech, FinTech, AgriTech, D2C, SaaS).
    *   AI generates a target customer persona based on the domain.
*   **Stage 1: The Idea**
    *   3 simulated WhatsApp-style conversations (Skeptical Customer, Domain Expert, Supportive Friend).
    *   Decision: Define the idea in one line.
*   **Stage 2: The Plan**
    *   Guided Business Model Canvas (6 fields).
    *   AI reality check on Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs. Pricing.
*   **Stage 3: The Team**
    *   Choose from 3 generated co-founder profiles (or go solo).
    *   Equity split negotiation.
    *   *Emotional check-in #1*
*   **Stage 4: The Build**
    *   Allocate ₹5,00,000 seed money across Product/Tech, Marketing, Hiring, and Operations.
    *   Outcome preview based on budget allocation.
*   **Stage 5: The Wall (The Most Important Stage)**
    *   Tone shifts to dark and serious: "Something has gone wrong."
    *   User faces 1 of 8 major crises based on earlier decisions (e.g., CTO quits, Competitor launches).
    *   *Emotional check-in #2* + Unlock a real BPT Founder Voice Note.
*   **Stage 6: The Grind**
    *   Simulated dashboard month-by-month for 6 months.
    *   One decision prompt + one event per month (e.g., top salesperson asks for a raise).
*   **Stage 7: The Crossroads**
    *   Decide the startup's fate: Raise funding (AI investor pitch), Bootstrap, Sell, or Shut down.
*   **Stage 8: The Outcome**
    *   Simulation debrief: Revenue reached, team built, key decisions.
    *   Founder Profile revealed (The Builder, Hustler, Strategist, Visionary, or Survivor).
    *   Emotional Resilience Score (0-100).
    *   Bridge to the BPT Community (convert players to community members).

### 2. Founder Passport
Generated immediately after Stage 8. A visually beautiful, shareable image (1080x1080px) containing:
*   Student's Name
*   Domain & Founder Type
*   Emotional Resilience Score
*   Their Key Decision & Startup Outcome

### 3. Guest Preview (No Login Required)
Stages 0 and 1 are playable without an account. This reduces friction so students can share a link and friends can start playing immediately. They hit a login wall at Stage 2.

### 4. Real Founder Voice Notes
Embedded inside the simulation. Real BPT founders share 30-45 second audio clips about the challenges they faced in the real world that mirror the simulation.

### 5. Freemium Gate (After Stage 4)
*   **FREE**: Stages 0 through 4 (up to "The Build").
*   **PREMIUM (₹299)**: Stages 5 through 8 (The Wall, Grind, Crossroads, Outcome) + Founder Passport + Voice Note unlocks.
