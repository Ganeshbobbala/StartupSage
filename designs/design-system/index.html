Overview

Welcome to the official StartupSage Design System. This document serves as the living source of truth for all designers and developers working on the platform.

Principles

Our design system is built to reflect the core values of experiential learning: modern, clean, accessible, and fast. Spacing is governed by a strict 8-point grid, corners are kept friendly with a 8–12px radius, and colors are chosen to maintain a warm but professional tone.

Inspiration

This design system draws visual aesthetics from industry-leading platforms:

  • Duolingo: Friendly elements, thick borders, badges, and progress feedback.
  • Coursera: Clean structured course grids, metadata lists, and cards.
  • Linear: Gorgeous dark mode gradients, fine borders, and minimal accents.
  • Notion: Simple grids, typography spacing, and functional inputs.
